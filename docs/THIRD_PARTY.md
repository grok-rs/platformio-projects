## What is *not* used

- **No Fritzing or KiCad graphics.** Their part and symbol libraries are under share-alike licences
  (CC BY-SA), so the site draws its own breadboard parts and uses its own symbol sprite instead.
- **No LMS (Beetroot Academy) text or images.** Lesson pages paraphrase the course material in their own
  words and link to the LMS and to the original YouTube videos.
- Datasheet numbers are facts with citations (`data/facts.json`); no datasheet figures are copied.
